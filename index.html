The Tournament

Sunday football,
Guwahati style

THE SUNDAY LEAGUE started as a way to get Guwahati's local footballers off the sidelines and onto the pitch. Every season not only bring new teams from across the neighbourhood but also bring new memories, new shining stars and a chance to prove themselves on the pitch.

The Sunday League is more than just another football tournament — it is a platform for players who rarely get the chance to showcase their talent on the bigger stage to finally have their moment. Built around the growth of grassroots football, the tournament gives local talent in the city the space to compete, improve, and shine with pride and joy.
